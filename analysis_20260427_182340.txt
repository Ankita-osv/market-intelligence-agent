THEME: Iran's Military Rebuilding Amid Ceasefire

ANALYSIS: Iran has reportedly leveraged a ceasefire to bolster its military capabilities, which signifies a strategic pause in hostilities that may lead to heightened regional tensions. This development is crucial as it could affect the balance of power in the Middle East and influence geopolitical relations, particularly between the U.S. and Iran. For U.S. markets, this might raise concerns about potential future conflicts, affecting investor sentiment in sectors such as defense and energy, potentially driving oil prices up. In India, heightened tensions could lead to increased oil prices, impacting inflation and long-term economic growth. Consequently, capital may flow into defensive investments and commodities like gold as investors seek security amid geopolitical uncertainty.
