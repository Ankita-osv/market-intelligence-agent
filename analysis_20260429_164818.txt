Smart money is likely moving into sectors that can benefit from both increasing oil prices and potential stabilization in geopolitical tensions. Look to see increased investments in companies leveraging AI to optimize energy consumption and logistics, as sustainable and tech-driven solutions become essential in a high-cost energy landscape. 

### 4. US vs INDIA IMPACT

In the U.S., the impending rate decision will have direct impacts on tech valuations, particularly with rising yields potentially depressing growth stocks. The energy sector could see a windfall from rising oil prices, but an overall inflationary sentiment could stoke concerns among consumers. In India, rising energy costs will likely throttle growth, especially for sectors dependent on energy imports; inflation will pressure the Reserve Bank of India to act cautiously. However, favorable oil prices could also incentivize investment in domestic infrastructure and technology to reduce dependence on imports.
