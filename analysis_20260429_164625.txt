In the U.S., rising interest rates will likely stymie growth in consumer spending, dampening equity market performance. Meanwhile, India, with its burgeoning tech landscape and favorable demographics, may attract capital flows, especially in AI and renewable energy sectors. However, the exchange rate instability with USD/INR at 94.82 could challenge import-dependent sectors. Thus, U.S. tech and commodities will face headwinds, while India could be an emerging outperformer.
